Consultation Information

A simple first step for discussing your legal matter with the firm.

A consultation is the first step in determining whether the firm can assist with your matter and what information may be needed next.

Before the consultation

  • Identify the legal issue and jurisdiction
  • Gather key dates and deadlines
  • Organize important documents
  • Write down the outcome you are seeking

During the consultation

Expect the discussion to focus on the basic facts, the legal issues that may be involved, jurisdiction, possible next steps and whether representation is a fit.
