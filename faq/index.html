Common questions

Which states is Ronald Brilliant licensed in?

The firm’s current site states that Ronald Brilliant is licensed in the state courts of California, Idaho and Nevada.

Can the firm handle immigration matters outside those states?

Yes. The current site describes a nationwide federal immigration practice. Whether the firm can take a particular case still depends on the facts and any applicable court or agency requirements.

What is the 2YouLaw Program™?

It is the firm’s mobile legal-assistance model. Many tasks may be handled by phone, email or video, while some matters may require in-person representation or travel.

Does the firm handle matters involving more than one area of law?

The firm’s stated philosophy is that legal problems often overlap. Its practice spans several areas of civil law so connected issues can be identified and discussed.

How do I request a consultation?

Use the consultation booking link or contact the firm by the phone number appropriate to your location or matter.

Can I rely on information on this website as legal advice?

No. Website information is general and does not create an attorney-client relationship. Legal advice depends on the facts, law and jurisdiction of a specific matter.
