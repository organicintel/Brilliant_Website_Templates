Wills, Trusts & Estates

Estate planning, administration and probate-related counsel.

Estate planning is about making important decisions in advance so family members are not left to make them during a crisis.

Estate-planning services identified by the firm

  • Wills and pour-over wills
  • Living and revocable trusts
  • Irrevocable and charitable trusts
  • Durable powers of attorney
  • Health-care directives
  • Estate administration
  • Guardianships and conservatorships
  • Probate litigation and defense

Planning in context
